About the Forum

A bridge across
ten years and eleven jurisdictions.

Since 2016, the Asia Privacy Bridge Forum has served as an annual meeting point for the researchers, regulators, and industry leaders shaping the privacy and security regimes of the Asia-Pacific.

Mission

Bridge the theory, practice, and governance of privacy in Asia.

The Asia Privacy Bridge Forum was founded on the premise that privacy is not a domestic project. Data crosses borders faster than the law that governs it, and the questions raised by new technologies — generative AI, autonomous systems, cross-jurisdictional platforms — outrun any one country's answers.

Each October, the Forum brings together the region's leading voices to translate research into policy, and policy into practice. The convening is deliberately compact: one day, two halls, and a program designed to produce the conversations that could not happen anywhere else.

Four Pillars

What the Forum does.

01

Convene the region

The Forum is the leading annual meeting point for privacy scholarship and practice in the Asia-Pacific — spanning Korea, Japan, Singapore, the Philippines, Australia, and the wider region, alongside dialogue partners from the EU and North America.

02

Publish and archive

Selected papers are peer-reviewed and archived. The Forum maintains a public record of every edition since 2016, tracing the evolution of the field's most consequential debates.

03

Bridge law and technology

Sessions are structured to bring regulators and technologists into the same room. The morning plenary is deliberately mixed; the afternoon parallels go deep on cross-disciplinary problems.

04

Train the next cohort

Graduate students at partner institutions serve as rapporteurs, co-authors, and session hosts — building the next generation of Asia-Pacific privacy leadership through participation, not observation.
