15th Edition · 16 October 2026

Autonomous Agents in Motion.

Securing resilience and accountability in AI & privacy. One day, two halls, a plenary morning and parallel afternoon — at the FKI Tower Conference Center in Yeouido, Seoul.

Theme

When systems act on their own, who answers when they fail?

The fifteenth edition convenes around a shift that has moved from lab to production in a single year: autonomous agents that plan, act, and delegate on our behalf. Three tracks structure the day's inquiry.

Track A

Agentic AI & Consent

Delegation, the human loop, and the shape of meaningful consent when a system negotiates on your behalf.

Track B

Cross-border Data

Interoperability of privacy regimes across APEC, EU, and Korean frameworks in an agent-mediated economy.

Track C

Audit & Assurance

Evidence, red-teaming, and the accountability record — how we prove what an autonomous system did, and why.

Program

Two halls, one bridge.

A plenary morning in the Grand Ballroom followed by parallel afternoon sessions in Ruby Hall. Program subject to change; final version will be published one week prior.
